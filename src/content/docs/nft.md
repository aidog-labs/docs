## NFT Overview

AIDOG NFTs are limited collectibles (maximum 1,000) with utility inside the ecosystem.

## How to Mint / Generate

NFTs are generated when a wallet’s net $AIDOG accumulation via Uniswap V4 Hooks crosses every 100,000 $AIDOG threshold.

Generation is first-come, first-served until the 1,000 supply cap is reached.

## Utility & Benefits

- Required to create strategies on StrategyHub
- Provides House Pool revenue boost based on quantity and rarity
- Future utility expansions possible

## Maintenance Requirements

Each NFT requires a minimum $AIDOG balance (or equivalent staked in House Pool) to remain active.

If the required balance is not maintained, the NFT may be burned.

## Rarity & Boost

NFT boost power in House Pool is determined by both the number of NFTs held and their rarity rank. Higher rarity NFTs provide stronger boosts.
