## Revenue Distribution

Protocol revenue is distributed primarily to House Pool stakers, with a portion allocated to the Buyback Fund and team operations.

## Buyback Fund

A portion of protocol revenue is allocated to a Buyback Fund.

This fund is used to periodically repurchase and permanently burn $AIDOG and/or NFTs, subject to DAO decisions.

## Fees Overview

- YieldMax: 5% of earned yield
- StrategyHub: Carry Fee (only on profits)
- House Pool withdrawal: 1%
- Other product-specific fees may apply
