## Connect Wallet

AIDOG currently runs on the Base network.

<Steps>

1. Click the “Connect Wallet” button in the top right corner.
2. Choose your preferred wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, etc.).
3. Make sure your wallet is switched to the Base network.
4. Approve the connection.

</Steps>

Once connected, you can interact with all AIDOG products.

## Supported Chains

- Primary Chain: Base
- Stocks (RWA): BNB Chain (BSC)
- Future support for additional chains may be added.

<Aside type="caution">
	Some products (such as Stocks) require assets on BNB Chain. You may need to
	bridge assets if necessary.
</Aside>

## Quick Start Guide

<Steps>

1. Connect your wallet on Base.
2. Acquire $AIDOG or USDC.
3. Explore the products:
   - Deposit into YieldMax for automated yield
   - Deposit into StrategyHub to follow or create strategies
   - Stake $AIDOG into House Pool to share protocol revenue
   - Use Stocks to trade tokenized equities
   - Try CycleVault for BTC cycle-based allocation

</Steps>
