## Audits

Smart contracts have been audited. Audit reports are available on the website.

## Risk Disclosure

Using AIDOG involves risks, including but not limited to:

- Smart contract risk
- Market volatility
- Liquidation risk (in Borrow)
- Impermanent loss or strategy underperformance
- Cross-chain bridge risks (where applicable)

<Aside type="danger">
	Users should only risk capital they can afford to lose.
</Aside>
