Most fees first become system revenue, then are split:

* 60% to House Pool
* 20% to official operations
* 20% to buy back $AIDOG and burn it

Some charges are not part of this split:

* StrategyHub creator carry
* House Pool’s 1% withdrawal fee, which is paid to remaining stakers
