### Operational Controls

AIDOG is a Web3 system, but it is not fully trustless in every layer.

The official team currently retains limited operational responsibility, including:

* maintaining frontends and product parameters that are not fully immutable
* using the 20% operations allocation to keep the project running
* possible emergency controls on a StrategyHub strategy if a creator is found to be acting against users

These controls exist because some products manage other people’s funds. 

Users should assume:

* official frontends can change
* contract upgrades, if any, depend on the specific product
* emergency action is a last resort, not a guarantee that losses will be reversed

### What AIDOG Does Not Guarantee

AIDOG does not guarantee:

* profit
* principal protection
* a rising $AIDOG price
* that a strategy creator will trade well
* that YieldMax APY will stay high
* that CycleVault signals will be correct
* that tokenized stocks can always be redeemed 1:1 for the traditional asset
* that buybacks will be large or frequent
* that emergency controls can recover funds in every case

Past performance does not guarantee future results.

### User Responsibility

Before using AIDOG, you should:

* connect only through official links
* confirm you are on the correct network
* understand the product you are depositing into
* check fees, lock-up rules, and withdrawal conditions
* size positions so that a full loss would not create hardship
* monitor StrategyHub holdings, House value, and Borrow-style health factors if those products are used later

Only use funds you can afford to lose.
