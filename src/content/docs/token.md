## Token Utility

$AIDOG is the core utility and ownership token of the AIDOG system.

Key utilities:

- Stake in House Pool to earn protocol revenue
- Used as collateral in certain products
- Required for NFT maintenance
- Governance participation (future)

## Tokenomics

- Total Supply: 1,000,000,000 $AIDOG
- Fully Circulating
- No inflation

## How to Acquire $AIDOG

$AIDOG can be acquired through:

- Swap on the Token page
- Decentralized exchanges (Uniswap on Base)
- Other supported markets
