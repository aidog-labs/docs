### What it is

House Pool is the shared-ownership layer of AIDOG. Users stake $AIDOG and receive a portion of system revenue.

Important distinction:

1. Fees charged by AIDOG products are system revenue.
2. System revenue is split as follows:

* 60% to House Pool
* 20% to the official operating budget, used to run and maintain the project
* 20% used to buy back $AIDOG and burn it

### Where system revenue comes from

System revenue is the protocol’s income before the 60 / 20 / 20 split. Current sources include:

* YieldMax: 5% of the interest users earn
* StrategyHub: the fixed 5% protocol portion of Carry Fee, charged only on profitable withdrawals
* CycleVault: 10% of profit, charged only on profitable withdrawals
* Other protocol fees if new products go live later

### How to use it

1. Open House Pool.
2. Stake $AIDOG, or use a supported asset if the page offers auto-swap into $AIDOG before staking.
3. Your pool share starts receiving House Pool’s 60% slice of system revenue.
4. Withdraw when you want to exit.

A 1% withdrawal fee is charged. That 1% is distributed to remaining stakers, not to the team and not to the treasury.

### Money flow

1. A product charges a protocol fee.
2. That fee becomes system revenue.
3. 60% of system revenue is sent to House Pool.
4. House Pool stakers share that 60% according to their staked $AIDOG.
5. 20% stays with the official team for operations.
6. 20% is used to buy $AIDOG and burn it.

When you stake:

* Your House income=House Pool inflow * Your House share %

When you withdraw $AIDOG from House Pool:

* Withdrawal fee=Withdraw amount * 1%
* You receive=Withdraw amount−Withdrawal fee

The 1% fee is allocated to remaining House stakers in proportion to their shares.

### Buyback and burn

20% of system revenue is used to buy $AIDOG on the market and burn it. Burned tokens are removed from circulating supply and cannot be minted again. Its purpose is to reduce $AIDOG supply as protocol revenue is generated.

Buyback size depends on actual system revenue. 

### Risks and controls

* System revenue itself is variable. It depends on YieldMax interest, StrategyHub profitable withdrawals, CycleVault profitable withdrawals, and other future fees.
* If protocol activity is low, House income can be small.
* The price of $AIDOG can fall.
* The 1% withdrawal fee is paid to remaining stakers.
* Smart-contract risk still applies.
* House Pool is not a fixed-yield product and does not guarantee profit.
