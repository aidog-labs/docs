### What is $AIDOG

$AIDOG is the utility and ownership token of the AIDOG system on Base.

It is not designed as a standalone betting chip. Its main role is to represent participation in the protocol: staking in House Pool, using AIDOG products, and voting on governance matters.

Holding $AIDOG does not guarantee profit. The token can rise or fall with market conditions. Users should treat it as a utility asset inside the AIDOG system, not as a promise of price appreciation.

### Tokenomics

* Network: Base
* Token address: 0x80394Ae69F14444605032a7f2D74c8AB7d16A51d
* Total supply: 1,000,000,000 $AIDOG
* Circulating supply: 1,000,000,000 $AIDOG
* Inflation: none
* Supply cap: fixed

There is no scheduled minting after launch. Protocol income does not create new $AIDOG. Fees collected by AIDOG products first become system revenue, then are distributed according to the protocol split.

### Utility

Current utilities include:

* House Pool staking
  Stake $AIDOG in House Pool to receive a share of House Pool’s income. House Pool receives 60% of system revenue. Your share depends on how much $AIDOG you stake relative to the total pool.
* Governance
  $AIDOG can be used as a voting credential for official proposals, including proposals related to treasury use, buybacks, or protocol parameters.
* Exchange asset inside AIDOG
  $AIDOG can be swapped against assets such as USDC, ETH, and VIRTUAL on the Token page and on supported DEX pools. 
* Liquidity provision
  Users can add $AIDOG liquidity on supported DEX pools, such as Uniswap on Base, and receive trading fees from that pool.

Holding $AIDOG in a wallet alone does not automatically pay protocol income. Income sharing requires staking in House Pool.

### Token Page

The Token page is the main place to manage $AIDOG inside the app. Typical functions include:

* View contract address, FDV, TVL, 24-hour change, and price chart
* Swap $AIDOG
* Adjust slippage
* Add or remove liquidity
* See your LP share and pool APR

The Token page uses available routing to complete swaps. Users still pay ordinary network gas and any slippage in the selected route.

### Risks

* $AIDOG can lose value.
* Smart-contract, market, and liquidity risks apply.
* Staking in House Pool is not principal-protected.
* Protocol revenue can decline in quiet markets.
* Official treasury decisions may not always include buybacks.

$AIDOG is a utility and ownership token for the AIDOG system. It is not a claim on guaranteed cash yield, and it is not designed as a short-term speculative instrument.
