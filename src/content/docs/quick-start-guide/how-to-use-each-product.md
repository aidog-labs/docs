### YieldMax

- Go to the YieldMax page.
- Choose USDC or ETH.
- Enter the amount you want to deposit.
- Confirm the transaction in your wallet.
- Your funds will begin earning yield automatically.
- You can withdraw at any time.

YieldMax charges a 5% fee only on the yield you earn. This fee goes to House Pool.

### StrategyHub

To follow a strategy:

- Go to the StrategyHub page.
- Browse available strategies or open a strategy detail page.
- Click Deposit.
- Enter the amount of USDC you want to invest.
- Confirm the transaction.

Your USDC will be managed by the strategy creator. You will receive shares of the strategy based on the current net asset value.

To create a strategy:

- You must first deposit at least 10,000 USDC of your own capital.
- Fill in the strategy name, description, and carry fee.
- Confirm creation.

The displayed Carry Fee is the total performance fee (AIDOG system 5% + Creator’s share). It is charged only when investors, including the creator, withdraw with a profit. No fee is charged if there is no profit.

### CycleVault

- Go to the CycleVault page.
- Review the current AI Cycle Score and allocation.
- Deposit USDC.
- The system will automatically allocate between cbBTC and USDC based on cycle signals.
- The USDC portion is deployed into YieldMax to continue earning yield.
- You can withdraw according to the product rules.

### Stocks

- Go to the Stocks page.
- Select the tokens you want to swap.
- Enter the amount.
- Compare the best route with alternative routes if shown.
- Confirm the swap.

Platform-issued stock tokens end with “A”, such as CRCLA. These tokens are designed to track the underlying asset on a 1:1 basis and are routed across multiple liquidity sources for better execution.

Note: Stocks assets are on BNB Chain. If you do not have funds on BSC, you will need to bridge assets first.

### House Pool

- Go to the House Pool page.
- Enter the amount of $AIDOG you want to stake.
- Confirm the transaction.
- You will begin receiving a share of protocol revenue.
- You can withdraw at any time. A 1% withdrawal fee is distributed to remaining stakers.

Staking $AIDOG in House Pool also counts toward the balance required to maintain AIDOG NFTs.

### \* Important Notes

- AIDOG products involve market risk, smart contract risk, and potential loss of capital.
- Past performance does not guarantee future results.
- StrategyHub strategies are actively managed by creators and may lose money.
- Always check the product page, fees, and risk notes before depositing.
- Only use funds you can afford to lose.
