## What is AIDOG

AIDOG is an on-chain private banking system designed to lower the barriers to advanced financial strategies. Through a suite of integrated products — StrategyHub, YieldMax, Borrow, Stocks, CycleVault, and House Pool — users can access automated yield, discretionary strategies, leveraged opportunities, and tokenized real-world assets without needing deep financial expertise. The system is built around the principles of transparency, capital efficiency, and aligned incentives between users and the protocol.

## Vision

AIDOG aims to become the leading on-chain private bank where anyone, regardless of financial background, can grow their capital through simple, powerful, and transparent tools. We believe the future of finance is permissionless, automated, and accessible. AIDOG exists to make that future usable today.
