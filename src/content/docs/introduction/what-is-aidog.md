AIDOG is an on-chain private banking system designed to make sophisticated financial strategies accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or expertise. Traditional finance often requires significant capital, specialized knowledge, and access to exclusive products. Decentralized finance, while open, can be fragmented, complex, and difficult to navigate. AIDOG bridges this gap by offering a unified, user-friendly platform that brings together multiple powerful tools under one system.The platform currently includes:



* StrategyHub — A marketplace for discretionary investment strategies where creators actively manage portfolios, and users can deposit capital to follow them.


* YieldMax — An intelligent yield aggregator that automatically allocates assets into the safest and highest-yielding opportunities across DeFi lending markets.


* Stocks — On-chain trading of tokenized real-world assets, primarily focused on equities, with smart routing for optimal execution.


* CycleVault — An AI-driven vault that dynamically allocates between Bitcoin and stablecoins based on real-time Bitcoin cycle signals.


* House Pool — The core ownership and revenue-sharing layer of the protocol, where $AIDOG holders can stake their tokens to participate in the system’s success.



At the center of the ecosystem is the $AIDOG token, which serves both as a utility token and a representation of ownership in the protocol. Through House Pool, token holders can share in the revenue generated across the entire system.AIDOG is built with a clear philosophy: financial tools should be powerful, yet simple enough for anyone to use. By combining automation, transparency, and aligned incentives, we aim to deliver an experience closer to a modern private bank — but fully on-chain and accessible to all.
